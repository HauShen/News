New York Times News
_______
Wanna get some breaking news from New York Times?

Here is an appliation allow to get your news, and send to someone as email letter.
_______

Used Technologies:

-Spring (Boot, Data, Security)

-JPA / Hibernate

-Gradle

-MYSQL

-React,CSS,JavaScript

_______

About application:

-User Authentication: Secure login and authentication for admin, news poster and reader.

-Customer Management: As admin, you can easily add, update, and delete user information.

-Creating News: As admin or news poster, you can get today news from New York Times.

-Email sending: You can send a New York Times news as an email letter.

-Feedback: When User click the like button in email, he can give a like in feedback page.
